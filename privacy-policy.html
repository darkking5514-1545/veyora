Veyora — Privacy Policy

App: Veyora Package: com.rhstudio.veyora Developer: RH Studio PK Country: Pakistan
Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RH Studio PK ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Veyora Android application (the "App"), package name com.rhstudio.veyora. Veyora is a simple notes app that lets you create, edit, and delete text notes directly on your device. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We designed Veyora to be as private as possible. The App does not require an account or registration, and we do not collect:

  • Your notes or their content — notes are stored only locally on your device
  • Your name, email address, or any account or profile information
  • Your contacts
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Any form of usage analytics or behavioral tracking operated by us

Because notes never leave your device and are not uploaded to any server, we have no ability to view, access, recover, or restore the notes you create in the App.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Veyora shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. After every few notes, the App may ask if you'd like to watch a short video ad to support the App — watching is entirely optional and never required to use the App's core features.

To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device and app information (e.g., device model, operating system version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., impressions, clicks, and completed views)

This data is collected and handled by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, which you can review at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We do not control how Google processes this data and encourage you to review Google's policy for full details.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized (interest-based) ads at any time from your device's settings (typically under Settings → Privacy/Ads or Google Settings → Ads).

4. App Permissions

Veyora requests only two permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. These permissions are used solely to load and display ads through Google AdMob and to check network connectivity before doing so. They are not used to transmit your notes or personal information.

5. How Information Is Used

Any information collected by Google AdMob, as described above, is used only to deliver and measure the performance of the optional rewarded ads shown in the App. We do not use this data for any purpose of our own, and we do not combine it with any other information.

6. Data Storage and Security

All notes you create are stored locally on your device's internal storage. They are not backed up to any cloud service or server operated by us. Because your notes remain on your device, you are responsible for the security of your device (for example, using a screen lock) to prevent unauthorized access to your notes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your notes with anyone because we never receive them. The only data shared with a third party is the advertising-related information described in Section 3, which is collected directly by Google AdMob in connection with the optional rewarded ads.

8. Children's Privacy

Veyora is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address the matter.

9. User Choices and Rights

  • You may delete any or all of your notes within the App at any time.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time, which will remove all locally stored notes and app data from your device.
  • You may control ad personalization and reset your advertising ID through your device's settings.
  • You may choose not to watch any rewarded video ads offered in the App, as they are entirely optional.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable requirements. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
